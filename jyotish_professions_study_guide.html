🎯 AmK (Amatyakarak) — The Career Indicator

The Amatyakarak (AmK) is the planet with the second-highest longitude (ignoring signs). It is the single most important indicator of career/profession in Jaimini system.

  • AmK aspected or conjunct with Dara Karaka (DK) = Rise in life (True)
  • AmK strong, in Kendra/3H/11H from Lagna = High-profile career
  • AmK in transit influencing MD/AD, or in 10H/11H from them = Time of first salary / career start
  • AMK and MK in 10th house = Official travelling / change in job
  • AMK + 8P + 5P in 10H = Change of profile with promotion
⚠️ Exam Trap
Q: "Strength and placement of AmK is an indicator of work profile" → Answer: TRUE
Q: "Connection of 3,9,12 houses with Venus" → Export / fashion designing / foreign work

🏛️ Houses for Professional Distress

Professional distress comes from interaction of L/LL, 10H/L, and AmK with the Trik houses (6, 8, 12) and their lords.

Distress Triggers to Memorise
• Dasha/AD/PD of planets that are combust, retrograde, in planetary war, or in Mrityu Bhaga

• Transit of dasha planets in 5, 6, 8, 12H or over retrograde/combust planets
• Saturn/Venus or Rahu/Venus periods = special caution (K.N. Rao teaching)

Universal Principles

Core rules that apply across all horoscope analyses for profession — memorise these perfectly.

⚡ The AmK Analysis System
How to identify the AmK planet ▼
The planet with the 2nd highest longitude (ignoring signs) = AmK. The AK has the highest longitude. Example: if planets are at 14°30', 12°30', 16°15', 29°40', 1°47'... AK = 29°40' planet; AmK = 16°15' planet.
AmK placement results ▼
• AmK in Kendra or 3H/11H from lagna = high-profile career
• AmK in Kendra or 3H/11H from AK in Navamsha = smooth, rewarding career
• AmK aspected by malefics + ill-placed = fall in career / distress
• AmK + MK in 10H = official travelling
• 8P + 5P + AMK in 10H = change of profile with promotion
First Salary timing ▼
AmK in transit should either: (a) influence the MD/AD lords, or (b) be in the 10th or 11th sign from MD/AD. This combination = time of first salary / job commencement.
📈 Rise in Profession — Key Indicators
  • Dashas of: LL, 10L, exalted planets, and AmK planet = favourable for professional rise
  • Planets associated with or aspected by AmK also produce favourable results
  • Jaimini Raja Yoga (AK + AmK + PK + DK + 5L interaction) = strength of career yoga
  • Yoga gives results as per its strength in Navamsha
  • The Dashamsha is the "road map" — LL and 10L in Dashamsha are most critical
The Triple Check Rule
For any career event: Check it in (1) Natal chart → (2) Navamsha → (3) Dashamsha.
If all three confirm = certain. If only natal = uncertain.
🎓 Education Principles
  • The two primary education planets: Jupiter and Mercury — placement in Kendra or Trine = good education
  • Saraswati Yoga: Jupiter, Venus, Mercury in houses 1/2/4/5/7/9/10 (jointly or singly)
  • Budh-Aditya Yoga: Sun + Mercury combination = excellent teacher/speaker/mathematician
  • Shankha Yoga: 5L and 6L in mutual Kendra + strong Lagna = educational competitiveness
  • D-24 (Chaturvimshamsha) = divisional chart for education; use only with reliable birth time
  • For competitive exams: Dasha of LL + AD of 5L + PD of 10L at 12th class final = gets desired result
⚠️ Exam: Jupiter in 2H or 5H in Mercury/Venus sign, connecting with Merc/Venus → gives…
Answer: Proficiency in art including music
🔮 Dashamsha (D-10) Analysis Rules
  • Dashamsha = the "road map" of profession — its ups, downs, shades and verities
  • LL and 10L together in D-10 lagna = thorough professional
  • 6H and 12H of D-10 empty = friction-free service, no early retirement
  • In D-10: examine Dhana Yoga (2/5/9/11 house lords interaction) for income level
  • Two vacant signs in D-10 (not counted in Dashamsha) hold the "Karmic key" to profession
  • Strong D-10 with 10L in good position = definitive confirmation of profession promise
⚖️ Strength Assessment Rules
  • Strong rashi = rashi supported by its own lord (by placement or aspect) + benefics in or aspecting it
  • Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga: debilitated planet's exaltation sign lord in Kendra from Moon or Lagna lord in Kendra from Lagna = cancellation + Raj yoga
  • Vargottama = planet in same sign in natal and Navamsha = strengthened; Lagna Vargottama = very strong
  • Benefics on either side of a sign (Shubha Kartari) = gain strength
  • For service vs. business: concentration of planets around 10H (10H to 3H) = strengthens 10H
